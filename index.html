Material: Discussion Text

1. Definition & Social Function

Discussion Text is a text which presents a problematic discourse. This problem will be discussed from different viewpoints (Pros and Cons).
Social Function: To present at least two points of view about an issue / to look at an issue from multiple perspectives before making a recommendation.

2. Generic Structure

  • Issue: Stating the issue which is to discussed.
  • Argument Pro (Supporting): Presenting the point in supporting the presented issue.
  • Argument Contra (Against): Presenting other points which disagree with the supporting point.
  • Conclusion / Recommendation: Stating the writer's recommendation of the discourse.

3. Language Features

  • Using Simple Present Tense (e.g., The school provides...).
  • Using Thinking Verbs (feel, hope, believe).
  • Using Contrastive Conjunctions (however, on the other hand, but, in other side, nevertheless).
  • Using Modalities (must, should, could, may).

Example: Homework - Good or Bad?

(Issue) There are a lot of discussions as to whether children should be given homework or not.

(Pro) Some people claim that children do enough work in school already... Additionally, homework helps children learn independently.

(Contra) On the other hand, parents and teachers argue that it is important to find out what children can do on their own without the help from the teacher.

(Conclusion) In conclusion, homework is good as long as it is not given too much.

The Smartphone Dilemma

In modern education, bringing smartphones to school has become a hot debate among parents, teachers, and students. Everyone seems to have a strong opinion about it.

Those who support the idea believe that smartphones are powerful learning tools. "With internet access, students can find information instantly," says Mr. Budi, an IT teacher. Furthermore, in emergencies, a phone allows students to contact their parents quickly, ensuring their safety.

However, on the other hand, many educators strongly oppose this. They argue that smartphones are a massive distraction. Students often play games or check social media during lessons instead of paying attention. Additionally, there are concerns about cyberbullying happening right inside the classroom.

Can you identify the Issue, Arguments For, Arguments Against, and think of your own Conclusion?
